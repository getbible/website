# Support the mission

Help Get Bible make Scripture accessible in more applications, communities and places. Donations support the continuing work of the project.

## Make a donation

Use the bank details on this page when available. If bank details are not displayed, email [getBible@TrueChristian.church](mailto:getBible@TrueChristian.church?subject=Supporting%20Get%20Bible) to request the current donation information directly from the team.

You can use the same address to discuss a donation or ask how your support can help. Do not send banking passwords, card details or other credentials by email.

## Support through your skills

You can also contribute code, testing, documentation, translation expertise or operational help. Visit [Get involved](/contribute/) to find a starting point, or explore the [projects](/project/) to see where your experience fits.

## Our affiliation

Get Bible is a project of [trueChristian.church](https://trueChristian.church). Read [our mission](/about/) to understand what we are building and why.

For public questions about the APIs, tools or applications, use the [Get Bible support desk](https://git.vdm.dev/getBible/support).
